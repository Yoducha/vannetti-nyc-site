Made in Italy, factory-direct

Our scarves are woven in Biella, at the foot of the Alps, by a family-run mill founded in 1947. The ties are made in Como, Italy's silk capital, and the gloves in Naples, by makers who have cut leather gloves for generations.

Vannetti buys factory-direct and sells to stores itself. One step between the maker and your shelf is why a 100% cashmere scarf can start at $52.90 wholesale — and why we can say yes to a first order of any size.
